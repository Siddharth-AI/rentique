Questions

Before you
book.

Anything else? Book a trial and the Rentique team will help you find the right look.

How do you make sure outfits are hygienic?

Every outfit is cleaned after each rental and delivered in sealed packaging. QR tracking on the seal makes cleanliness visible and checkable.

What if the outfit doesn't fit?

Try it at a trial hub or use our size guidance before you book. If there's a problem close to the event, last-minute replacement support helps you find another look.

How long can I keep an outfit?

Rentals start from 3 days, which covers the event plus delivery and pickup. Longer durations can be chosen when you book.

How do delivery and return work?

Your outfit arrives at your door in sealed packaging. After the event we pick it up, with the option to rent again for your next occasion.

Where is Rentique available?

We are starting in Indore. Metro cities come next, followed by a digital catalogue at national scale.

What does it cost?

Most outfits rent for ₹500–₹3,000 depending on the piece, a fraction of what you would pay to buy the same look.
